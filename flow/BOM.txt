THE SHORT VERSION OF WHAT IS MISSING
  Every moving part. The frame, carriage, collars and cap are not
  printed yet, and not one piece of the motion hardware is here:
  no screw, no nut, no rods, no LM8UU, no 608, no coupler. The
  electronics are done and proven, the mechanism does not exist.

  Two items decide whether the rig works at all rather than
  merely existing, and both are easy to order wrong:
    T8x8 lead screw.  T8x2 looks identical and is sold far more
                      often. At a 2 mm lead the same beat needs
                      919 rpm, which no 12 V NEMA 17 will do.
    Qosina 9105x check valve.  The luer-to-luer alternative cracks
                      at 0.433 psi, which is the entire head.
